Fresh Start questions

Who is the Fresh Start Program for?

Business owners whose credit is keeping them from the funding they want right now, whether a lender said no or you think they might.

Does it cost anything to talk to a specialist?

No. The first consultation is free and there's no obligation. Your specialist explains any program costs in writing before you decide.

Will this hurt my credit score?

No. Booking a call and reviewing your options doesn't affect your credit score.

Can you guarantee I'll qualify?

No one honestly can. What we do is show you exactly what each option looks for and give you a plan to get there. Many owners also qualify for revenue-based options right away.

How long does it take?

It depends on your profile. Your specialist gives you a realistic timeline on the first call.

Do I need an attorney?

Most people don't. If our review finds that something on your credit report may have violated your rights, we can connect you with a consumer protection attorney to look at your case.

The Fresh Start Program is a consultation and planning service. Results vary, and we don't guarantee any specific credit score, approval or funding.
