Frequently asked questions

How much funding can my business get?

Our lending partners fund from small working-capital amounts up to $5 million. What you qualify for depends on your revenue, time in business, credit and the type of funding.

How fast can I get funded?

Some options, like merchant cash advances and lines of credit, can fund in as little as one to two business days after approval. SBA loans and home equity lines take longer, usually several weeks.

What do I need to apply?

Basic business information and your three most recent business bank statements. Some programs ask for tax returns or other documents; your advisor will tell you exactly what's needed.

Do I need good credit?

No. Credit is one factor, but many of our options look mostly at your revenue and bank deposits. We work with a wide range of credit profiles, and there's no minimum score to apply. If your credit is holding you back, our Fresh Start Program gives you a step-by-step plan to get funding-ready.

Are you a lender?

No. Secure Biz Solutions is a funding broker. We match you with lending partners in our network, and the lender makes the final credit decision.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free, and you're never obligated to accept an offer.

How long does my business need to be open?

Most programs look for at least six months in business. Equipment financing and some other programs may work for newer businesses.

What happens after I apply?

Your advisor calls you, usually the same business day during business hours, to review your options. You choose what fits, and the lender handles final approval and funding.

Paying too much to take cards?

Our Zero-Fee Processing program can cut what you pay to accept credit cards. Send us a recent processing statement and we'll show you, line by line, what you could save.
