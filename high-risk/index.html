Frequently asked questions

What makes a business high-risk?

Lenders call an industry high-risk when they see more chargebacks, regulation or ups and downs in revenue. It's about the industry, not about you.

Will I pay more?

Sometimes. Costs depend on the lender and your numbers. Your advisor shows you the full cost of every offer before you choose.

Do you work with CBD and vape businesses?

Yes, through lending partners that serve those industries, subject to their approval.

What do I need to apply?

The same as any applicant: basic business details and your three most recent bank statements.

Ready to get funded?

One short application. A dedicated advisor. Real options in front of you.
