Frequently asked questions

What is Zero-Fee Processing?

It's a compliant pricing program where a small, clearly shown fee on card payments covers your processing cost, so you keep more of each sale. Your advisor explains exactly how it works for your business before you sign up.

Is it legal?

Yes, when it's set up correctly and disclosed to customers. We set it up to meet card-network rules and your state's requirements.

Do I have to switch to get a review?

No. The review is free and there's no obligation.

What statement should I send?

Your most recent full monthly statement from your current processor, as a PDF or a clear photo.
