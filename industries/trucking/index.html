The challenge

In trucking, cash goes out before it comes in. Fuel, insurance and repairs are due now, while brokers and shippers take 30 to 60 days to pay.

A single breakdown can park a truck and stop your income. The right funding keeps loads moving while you wait to get paid.

What trucking owners use funding for

Truck purchase

Add a truck or replace an aging one.

Trailer purchase

Major repairs

Fuel and operating cash

Fleet expansion

$150K–$500K

Frequently asked questions

Can I finance a used truck?

Yes, from dealers and often from private sellers.

Can new authorities qualify?

Some programs work with newer authorities, especially with good personal credit or a down payment.

How does factoring help truckers?

You get paid for delivered loads within days instead of waiting on brokers.

Can I get funding with bad credit?

Often, yes. Equipment financing and factoring weigh the truck and your customers more than your score.

How fast can I get funded?

Factoring and lines of credit can fund within days. Truck financing often takes two to five business days.
