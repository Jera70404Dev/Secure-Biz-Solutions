Salon and med spa funding

Aesthetic devices, buildouts and expansion. Funding to grow your practice without draining your cash.

See My Funding Options

Our team has helped 10K+ businesses secure $100 million+ in funding.

The challenge

A single laser or body-contouring device can cost more than a year of profit, and the newest treatments are what bring clients in. Buildouts for treatment rooms add up fast.

Paying cash slows your growth. Financing lets the new equipment pay for itself as you book treatments.

What salon and med spa owners use funding for

Aesthetic devices

Lasers, body contouring and skin devices.

Buildout or renovation

Frequently asked questions

Can I finance an aesthetic device?

Yes. Devices are one of the most common things we fund for med spas.

Do you fund salons as well as med spas?

Yes. Hair salons, nail salons, barbershops, day spas and med spas.

Can a new practice qualify?

Equipment financing may work for newer businesses, especially with good personal credit.

Can I finance a buildout?

Yes, with a term loan or SBA loan, depending on the size and timeline.

How fast can I get funded?

Equipment financing often funds in two to five business days after approval.
