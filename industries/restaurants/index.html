The challenge

Restaurants run on thin margins and uneven weeks. A walk-in cooler dies, a slow month hits, or a renovation can't wait, and the cash isn't there.

Many banks see restaurants as risky and won't lend. Our partners look at your actual sales and deposits instead.

Frequently asked questions

Do banks lend to restaurants?

Many are cautious with restaurants. Our lending partners look at your sales and deposits instead.

Can I get funding for a new restaurant?

Most programs need six months of sales. Equipment financing may work sooner.

Can I repay from my card sales?

Yes. With a merchant cash advance, payments can come from a share of your daily card sales.

Can I finance used kitchen equipment?

Yes, in many cases.

Do you fund bars?

Yes. Bars, breweries and nightlife businesses can qualify, subject to lender approval.
