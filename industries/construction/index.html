Our team has helped 10K+ businesses secure $100 million+ in funding.

The challenge

Contractors pay for crews and materials up front, then wait on draws and slow-paying general contractors. Retainage can hold back 5 to 10% of your money for months.

Winning a bigger job can make the cash crunch worse. The right funding lets you say yes to the work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get funding to start a big job?

Yes. A line of credit or term loan can cover materials and payroll before the first draw.

Can I factor progress billings?

Some factoring programs accept them. Your advisor will match you with one that does.

Can I finance used equipment?

Yes, from dealers, auctions and often private sellers.

Do subcontractors qualify?

Yes. Subcontractors are among the businesses we fund most often.

How fast can I get funded?

Lines of credit and factoring can fund within days. Equipment financing often takes two to five business days.
