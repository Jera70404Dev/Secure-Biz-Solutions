How equipment financing works

Equipment financing pays for new or used equipment, and the equipment itself secures the loan. Because the lender has that collateral, it's often easier to qualify for than other loans, even for newer businesses.

You make fixed payments over two to seven years, often matched to how long the equipment lasts. At the end of the term, the equipment is yours.

Why business owners choose equipment financing

  • Easier approval: the equipment is the collateral.
  • Keep your cash: little or no money down on many deals.
  • New or used: finance from a dealer or a private seller.
  • Fixed payments: budget-friendly terms of two to seven years.
  • Possible tax benefits: ask your tax advisor about deductions on equipment purchases.

Frequently asked questions

What can I finance?

Trucks, trailers, construction equipment, kitchen equipment, medical and spa devices, machinery, computers and more.

Can I finance used equipment?

Yes, from dealers and often from private sellers.

How much can I get?

From $5,000 up to $5 million.

Do I need a down payment?

Many deals need little or nothing down. Some need up to 20%, depending on credit and the equipment.

Can a new business qualify?

Some programs fund businesses under six months old, especially with good personal credit.

How long are the terms?

Two to seven years, usually matched to the equipment's useful life.

How fast can I get funded?

Often two to five business days after approval.

Who owns the equipment?

You do. The lender holds a lien until the loan is paid off.

What do I need to apply?

Your bank statements and a quote or invoice for the equipment.

Is this a lease?

It can be either. Your advisor will explain the loan and lease options and which fits you better.

Ready to get funded?

One short application. A dedicated advisor. Real options in front of you.
