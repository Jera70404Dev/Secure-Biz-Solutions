What a business credit card is

A business credit card gives you a revolving limit for everyday purchases: supplies, fuel, software, travel and advertising. Paid in full each month, it costs nothing in interest.

Your advisor helps you find cards that match your credit and spending, including cards with rewards or introductory offers when you qualify.

Why business owners choose business credit cards

  • Separate your finances: keep business spending off your personal cards.
  • Build business credit: on-time payments build your business credit history.
  • Rewards: many cards earn cash back or points on business spending.
  • Employee cards: set limits for team members.

Frequently asked questions

Can a new business get a business credit card?

Yes. Approval is usually based on your personal credit, so new businesses often qualify.

What credit score do I need?

Most cards look for 680 or higher. Some cards work with lower scores and lower limits.

Does it affect my personal credit?

Applying usually involves a personal credit check, and some issuers report to personal credit. Your advisor will explain each card's terms.

How high is the limit?

Usually $5,000 to $100,000 or more, depending on your credit and revenue.

Do I need an EIN?

No. Sole proprietors can apply with their Social Security number.

Can I get cards for employees?

Yes, most business cards offer employee cards with spending limits you set.

Are there 0% intro offers?

Some cards offer them to qualified applicants. Terms change often, so your advisor shows what's available now.

How long until I get the card?

Usually one to two weeks after approval.

Does it build business credit?

Many issuers report to business credit bureaus, which helps build your business profile.

Should I use a card or a loan?

Cards are best for everyday spending you pay off monthly. For larger amounts, a loan or line of credit usually costs less.
