What an SBA loan is

SBA loans are made by approved lenders and partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. That guarantee lets lenders offer longer terms and lower rates than most other business loans.

The most common program, the SBA 7(a) loan, goes up to $5 million. SBA Express is a faster version for amounts up to $500,000. SBA loans take longer to close than other options, so they're best when you can plan ahead.

Why business owners choose sba loans

  • Low rates: rates are capped by SBA rules.
  • Long terms: up to 10 years for working capital and equipment, and up to 25 years for real estate.
  • Lower payments: longer terms keep each payment smaller.
  • Flexible use: working capital, equipment, real estate, buying a business or refinancing.
  • SBA Express: a faster track for amounts up to $500,000.

Frequently asked questions

What is an SBA loan?

A loan from an approved lender that's partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration.

How much can I borrow?

SBA 7(a) loans go up to $5 million. SBA Express goes up to $500,000.

How long does it take?

Usually 30 to 90 days from application to funding. SBA Express can be faster.

What credit score do I need?

Most SBA lenders look for 650 or higher.

How long do I need to be in business?

Usually at least two years.

Do I need a down payment?

Often about 10%, depending on the loan and how you use it.

Is a personal guarantee required?

Yes. The SBA requires anyone owning 20% or more of the business to guarantee the loan.

What documents do I need?

Business and personal tax returns, financial statements, bank statements and a debt schedule. Your advisor gives you a checklist.

What can I use it for?

Working capital, equipment, real estate, buying a business or refinancing existing debt.

What if I need money faster?

Your advisor can line up a faster option, like a term loan or line of credit, while your SBA loan is in process.

One short application. A dedicated advisor. Real options in front of you.
